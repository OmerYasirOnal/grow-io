Version 1.4: our biggest update yet.

• CHAPTERS: 7 themed maps (Meadow, Desert, Glacier, Jungle, Volcano, Abyss, Galaxy). Complete the goals to unlock the next one: eat eggs from bird nests, collect gems, grow, hunt rivals and, in the last chapter, defeat the King.
• HERO SKINS: Samurai, Orbit, Kraken and Jet, each with a small power. Rivals sometimes wear special costumes too.
• 10 NEW SKINS: Glacier, Ocean, Zebra, Ember, Bubblegum, Tiger, Galaxy, Sakura, Venom and Inferno (38 skins in total).
• LIVELIER RIVALS: every game rolls a different field. Some bots are sharp, some are clumsy, and sometimes everyone is good. Giants, the King and the Boss now arrive smaller and grow into a fight with you.
• NEW ON THE MAP: treasure, food rain, arrow pads, mud and brake pads; edge arrows show where the action is.
• COMBO METER and FAIR BONUS ZONES (x1.2 to x2.5).
• FIXES: the game could freeze when the King and the Boss were both on the map; a shield now blocks exactly one hit; food no longer vanishes on screen; smoother haptics.
• A new launch animation, a new shop and settings with volume sliders and a language choice.

Thanks for playing. Grow on!
