Snake Grow.io is a neon snake arena game. Eat glowing food, grow longer, cut off your rivals and become the biggest snake on the map. Play anywhere, even without Wi-Fi.

HOW IT WORKS
• Steer with one thumb and tap BOOST for a burst of speed.
• Eat food to grow. Make rivals crash into your body to take their length.
• Grow big enough to take down the King and the Boss for huge rewards.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 3 modes: Classic (endless), Rush (90 seconds) and Boss Rush.
• 12 AI rivals with player-style names that cut you off, surround you and race you for power-ups.
• The King: +500 score and 75 coins. The Boss: +1000 score and 100 coins.
• 4 power-ups: Speed, Shield, Magnet and Ghost.
• Bonus zones from x1.2 to x2.5 and a combo meter that makes you grow up to twice as fast.
• Map events: treasure, food rain, arrow speed pads, mud and brake pads.

CHAPTERS (NEW)
• 7 themed maps: Meadow, Desert, Glacier, Jungle, Volcano, Abyss and Galaxy.
• Goals unlock the next chapter: eggs from bird nests, gems, length, kills and the King.

HERO SKINS (NEW)
• Samurai: a blade that slices rival tails.
• Orbit: orbs that block a hit.
• Kraken: claws that grab a smaller rival next to your head.
• Jet: a jet that saves boost fuel.

COLLECT AND PROGRESS
• 38 snake skins (4 heroes) and 5 trails, with clear rarities and prices.
• Daily missions, daily login rewards, levels, rank tiers and 10 achievements.
• Your best scores are saved on your device.

POLISHED
• Smooth play and volume sliders for sound and music.
• Turkish and English.

Grow on and rule the arena!
